# HAR3015: a result is neither a rowset nor JSON, or both

**Error.** Found reading the config file. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A declared result is neither a rowset nor JSON, or both.

## Why haruspex reports it

A result is one or the other: rows, or one JSON document.

## What to do

Give `rowset` (the schema of one row) or `json` (the schema of the document), not both.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:115: error HAR3015: procedures["lib.p"].results: must have either "rowset" or "json", the schema of a row or of the JSON document.
```
