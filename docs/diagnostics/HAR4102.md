# HAR4102: an override of a procedure that isn't selected

**Error.** Found applying the config file to what was inferred. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

An override of a procedure the file's `schemas` don't select.

## Why haruspex reports it

Overrides apply to the procedures the file types; one of a procedure left out does nothing.

## What to do

Select the procedure, or remove the override. A declaration of `results` alone, with `why`, may be given for any
procedure, selected or not. A run with `--include` replaces the file's includes, and skips the overrides of procedures it
leaves out rather than reporting them.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:97: error HAR4102: procedures["lib.purge"]: the procedure lib.purge is not selected, so it can't be overridden. Overrides apply only to the procedures its schema's "include" selects and "exclude" leaves in.
```
