# HAR3110: a key both optional and nullable

**Error.** Found reading the config file. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A key that may be both left out and null.

## Why haruspex reports it

`FOR JSON` leaves a null out, so the two can't be told apart, and haruspex can't express both yet.

## What to do

Make the key required, or not nullable.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:166: error HAR3110: procedures["lib.p"].results.json.properties.a: may be both left out and null, which haruspex can't express yet: FOR JSON leaves a null out, so make the key required, or not nullable.
```
