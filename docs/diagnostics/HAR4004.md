# HAR4004: a schema selects nothing

**Error.** Found applying the config file to what was inferred. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A schema's `exclude` removes every procedure its `include` selects.

## Why haruspex reports it

A block that types nothing is as silent as a typo.

## What to do

Narrow the exclude, or remove the block.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:46: error HAR4004: schemas.lib: the schema "lib" selects nothing: its "exclude" removes every procedure its "include" selects. Narrow the exclude, or remove the block.
```
