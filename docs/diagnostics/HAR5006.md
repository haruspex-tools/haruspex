# HAR5006: a procedure's parameters' name is taken

**Error.** Found emitting the Zod module. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A procedure's parameters are declared under its name with Params after it (the parameters of `GetOrder` are
`GetOrderParams`, and their schema `GetOrderParamsSchema`), and what its `OUTPUT` parameters hold with Output after it
(those of `RingUp` are `RingUpOutput`); and another procedure or a named type already has that name.

## Why haruspex reports it

A module can't declare one name twice, and haruspex never renames anything on its own, so that adding an object can't change
an existing name.

## What to do

Give one of the two another name in the config file, with a `name` override or a naming rule.

## Example

```text
haruspex: error HAR5006: The parameters of shop.get_order are declared as GetOrderParams, which is also the name of another procedure or type; rename one of them in the config file.
```
