# HAR3006: a pattern isn't well formed

**Error.** Found reading the config file. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A pattern of procedure names isn't well formed: it is empty, or it has a `[` without its `]`, or a key of `procedures`
isn't a two-part name.

## Why haruspex reports it

A pattern haruspex can't read selects nothing it can say.

## What to do

Correct the pattern. `*` matches any characters within a part, `?` one; brackets make what they hold literal (`[a*b]`).

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:67: error HAR3006: schemas.lib.include[0]: '[get' has a '[' without its closing ']'.
```
