# HAR3020: an override says nothing

**Error.** Found reading the config file. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A path override that sets nothing: no `name`, `nullable`, `type` or `values`.

## Why haruspex reports it

An override that does nothing is probably one half written.

## What to do

Give what it overrides, or remove it.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:133: error HAR3020: procedures["lib.get_book"].paths["[].note"]: says nothing to override: give "name", "nullable", "type" or "values".
```
