# HAR3102: a rowset's required leaves a column out

**Error.** Found reading the config file. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A rowset's `required` leaves a column out.

## Why haruspex reports it

Every column of a rowset is in every row; a column can be null, but never missing.

## What to do

List every column in `required`, or leave `required` out.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:233: error HAR3102: procedures["lib.p"].results.rowset.required: every column of a rowset is always there, so "required" must list them all, or be left out.
```
