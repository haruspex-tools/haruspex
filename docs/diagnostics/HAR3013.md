# HAR3013: a why with no claim to explain

**Error.** Found reading the config file. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A `why` with no claim beside it to explain.

## Why haruspex reports it

A reason for nothing is probably a claim that was removed, or a reason in the wrong place.

## What to do

Move the `why` to the claim it explains, or remove it.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:134: error HAR3013: procedures["lib.get_book"].paths["[].title"]: "why" explains a claim, but there is none here: give "nullable", "type" or "values".
```
