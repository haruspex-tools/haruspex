# HAR6006: an --include pattern isn't well formed

**Error.** Found on the command line, or reading and writing files. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

An `--include` pattern isn't well formed: not a two-part name, or a `[` without its `]`.

## Why haruspex reports it

A pattern haruspex can't read selects nothing it can say.

## What to do

Write `schema.name`, with `*` and `?` within a part.

## Example

```text
haruspex: error HAR6006: invalid --include pattern: 'a.b.c' is not a two-part name such as sales.get_order, sales.* or *.list_*.
```
