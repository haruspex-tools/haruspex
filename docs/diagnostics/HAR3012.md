# HAR3012: a setting needs another beside it

**Error.** Found reading the config file. It stops the run: nothing is written.

## What it means

A setting that needs another beside it: a naming replacement's `pattern` and `with`, a path override's `type` and
`import`.

## Why haruspex reports it

One without the other says half of what it means.

## What to do

Add the setting the message names.

## Example

```text
haruspex.json:1:134: error HAR3012: procedures["lib.get_book"].paths["[].extra"]: "type" needs "import": the module that exports the schema.
```
